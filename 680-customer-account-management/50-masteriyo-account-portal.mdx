## How to Request Access to Your Account?

1. Go to [masteriyo.com/account](https://masteriyo.com/account), or click **Account** in the top navigation on any masteriyo.com page.

   ![Masteriyo account login page](https://mlcucdwjz8hi.i.optimole.com/w:auto/h:auto/q:auto/id:3f11665efe360c2af9818a31bb183168/directUpload/masteriyo-account-portal.png)

2. Enter the email address you used when you purchased Masteriyo.
3. Click **Send Access Link**.

<Callout>

**Note:** Use the exact email address you checked out with. If you're not sure which address that was, check your inbox for a receipt from Masteriyo around your purchase date, or [contact support](https://masteriyo.com/contact/).

</Callout>

## How to Open Your Account and View Your License?

1. Open your mailbox and check for the access link email — it usually arrives within a few minutes.
2. Click the link in the email to open your account page. It shows:
   - **Your purchase** — plan name and status (Active, Expired, etc.)
   - **Email** and **License Key**, with a **Copy** button next to the license key
   - **Renew** date — when your subscription is next due to bill
   - **View Receipt** — your original purchase invoice
   - **Manage Subscription** — update payment details, change plan, or cancel

   ![Masteriyo account page showing purchase details with license key](https://mlcucdwjz8hi.i.optimole.com/w:auto/h:auto/q:auto/id:f90892b07db1e12a8078b5c35dced5a3/directUpload/thank-you-page.png)

## How to Download the Latest or a Previous Version of Masteriyo Pro?

Your account page lists every released version of Masteriyo Pro, not just the latest — each with its own **Download** button. The most recent version is marked **Latest**.

![Masteriyo Pro downloads list showing multiple versions with Download buttons](https://mlcucdwjz8hi.i.optimole.com/w:auto/h:auto/q:auto/id:7fc371ac846d2f99bd2a0e99745fb7c0/directUpload/masteriyo-version-download.png)

Use this if you need to roll back to a previous version while troubleshooting a compatibility issue, instead of only ever having access to the newest release.

<Callout>

**Note:** Want to know how to install Masteriyo Pro? Check this [documentation](https://docs.masteriyo.com/getting-started/installation#installing-masteriyo-lms-pro-version).

</Callout>

## What to Do If You Didn't Receive the Access Link Email?

1. Check your spam or promotions folder.
2. Confirm you entered the exact email used at checkout. A typo, or a different address (e.g. a work email vs. a personal email), will not receive the link.
3. Still nothing after a few minutes? [Contact support](https://masteriyo.com/contact/) with your purchase email and approximate purchase date.
