Enabling this feature allows you to add unlimited lesson materials and gives students quick access to download or preview these materials.

### How to Use?

![enabling-download-material-in-masteriyo](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/24367265/195080532-85a1674b-2fd4-4f67-a24e-2886da4b440f.png)

1. Install [Masteriyo LMS](https://docs.masteriyo.com/getting-started/installation)
2. Activate the [Download Material Add-on](https://docs.masteriyo.com/getting-started/activate-masteriyo-addons)
3. Go to your **Dashboard**, navigate **Masteriyo**->**Courses**
4. Click on **Builder** option from the top
5. Select the **Lesson** where you want to add course material
6. Upload documents on **Download Materials**
7. Click the **Update**

![displaying-download-material-in-masteriyo](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/24367265/195080632-656902d7-cdc3-40ad-bb2c-0e403b8bba10.png)

### Download Materials on Assignment

- Activate the Masteriyo Assignment add-on and Download Material add-on.

You will be able to upload download materials on the assignments just like on the lessons.

![download-material-in-assignment](https://github.com/Masteriyo/masteriyo-docs/assets/75772539/0b6d98fa-30f7-402c-9d7e-4ae73e0b0608)

### Protected Downloads [Pro]

<Callout>
With Masteriyo Pro, lesson download materials are access-controlled — only enrolled students (along with instructors and admins) can download them. For restricted courses, files are served through secure, expiring links instead of public URLs. In the free version, attached materials use direct media URLs.
</Callout>
