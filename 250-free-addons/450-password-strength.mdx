Password Strength allows users to have a strong password with a combination of numbers, capital letters, and unique symbols while signing up.


## Prerequisites
1. Install [Masteriyo LMS](/getting-started/installation). Password Strength is built into Masteriyo as a core feature — no separate add-on activation is needed.

![settin-up-password-strength-with-mastriyo](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/24367265/189648775-2e96ceb1-7bf5-4f22-972d-80fe25992ef7.png)

## Configuration
1. Go to your **Dashboard**, navigate to **Masteriyo**> **Settings**.
2. Click on the **Advanced**->**Password Strength**.
3. Click on the **Save Settings**.

![displaying-password-strength-with-masteriyo](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/24367265/189648517-046dfbbd-aa66-4f60-ada7-c14cf7f66919.png)
