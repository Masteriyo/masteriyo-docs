This feature allows teachers to share upcoming courses and content with their users.

## Installation

Courses Coming Soon is a built-in feature of Masteriyo LMS — it works out of the box and does not require any add-on activation.

## Configuration

Go to **Masteriyo > Courses > Edit/create a course**. Now, follow the steps below.

- Go to the **Settings** tab and select **Schedule & Access** from the left panel.

![enable-course-coming-soon](https://img.masteriyo.com/w:auto/h:auto/q:auto/id:fd2535cff3e1fec4a0e3b6b00fe6459f/directUpload/enable-course-coming-soon.png)

Under **Course Availability**, enable the **Course Coming Soon** toggle to mark the course as upcoming. This reveals the following options:

- **Enrollment Date** - Set the date and time when enrollment for the course will open.
- **Hide Meta Data** - Enable this to hide the course meta data (such as duration, lessons, and level) while the course is in coming soon mode.
- **Hide Date Text** - Enable this to hide the text that displays the date the course will be available.
- **Set Course End Date** - Enable this to set a date after which the course will no longer be available.

Once saved, the course displays the coming soon status on the frontend, as shown below:
