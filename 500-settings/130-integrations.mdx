## Integrations

![Integrations](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/75772539/228790283-443c410c-d8b8-4664-87f6-5d33c5f1dfa3.png)

### WooCommerce

- **Unenrollment Status** - List of WooCommerce order statuses for which the students should be unenrolled.
- **Enable Add to Cart** - Show the WooCommerce Add to Cart flow for courses.
- **Auto Redirect to Courses** - Redirect customers to their courses after purchase.

### Easy Digital Downloads [Pro]

- **Unenrollment Status** - List of Easy Digital Downloads order statuses for which the students should be unenrolled.
