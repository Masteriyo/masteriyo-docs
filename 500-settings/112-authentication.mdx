<Callout> Location: Masteriyo > Settings > Authentication</Callout>

The Authentication tab groups the settings that control how users sign in and how their login is secured. Each section below corresponds to a sub-tab within Masteriyo > Settings > Authentication.

### Email Verification

- **Enable Email Verification** — Default: **On**. When enabled, users must verify their email address before they get full access to your site.

### QR Login

- **Enable QR Login** — Default: **Off**. Lets users log in via a QR code or login link generated from their account profile.
- **Attention Message** — A text field shown alongside the QR login. Default: *"Attention: Possession of the QR code or login link grants login access to anyone."*

<Callout>Because anyone holding the QR code or login link can sign in, keep the attention message in place so users understand the risk of sharing it.</Callout>

### Limit Login Session

- **Maximum Active Sessions** — Default: **0** (unlimited). Caps the number of concurrent device logins allowed per user. Set a positive number to limit how many devices can stay signed in at once.

### reCAPTCHA

This sub-tab appears only when the free **Google reCAPTCHA** add-on is activated under Masteriyo > Settings > Addons. It lets you protect your login and registration forms from bots and spam.

For setup and configuration details, see the [Google reCAPTCHA](/free-addons/google-recaptcha) add-on documentation.

### Two-Factor Authentication [Pro]

Adds a one-time password (OTP) step on top of the normal login. Available with Masteriyo Pro.

- **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** — Turns the OTP challenge on or off.
- **Location** — Where the second factor is enforced: Masteriyo & WP Login, Masteriyo login only, or WP login only.
- **OTP Length** — Number of digits in the one-time code. Default: **6**.
- **OTP Expiration** — How long a code stays valid. Default: **30 minutes**.
- **Resend Interval** — Minimum wait before a user can request a new code. Default: **2 minutes**.
- **Resend Max Attempts** — Maximum number of times a user can request a new code. Default: **10**.

For full setup instructions, see the [Two-Factor Authentication](/premium-addons/two-factor-authentication) add-on documentation.

### Social Login [Pro]

Lets users sign in with their existing social accounts. This sub-tab appears when the Social Login add-on is active.

- **Google** — Toggle Google sign-in and enter the Client ID and Client Secret.
- **Facebook** — Toggle Facebook sign-in and enter the Client ID and Client Secret.

For full setup instructions, see the [Social Login](/premium-addons/social-logins) add-on documentation.
