---
title: License Key Has Reached the Activation Limit
excerpt: Fix the "license key has reached the activation limit" error by deactivating your license in the Customer Dashboard and reactivating it on your site.
---

If you see the message "License key has reached the activation limit," it may be because the site was reset or a backup was restored. To fix this issue, please follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Log in to your [Customer Dashboard](https://docs.masteriyo.com/customer-account-management/customer-dashboard-manage-site-license#how-to-log-in-to-the-customer-dashboard) using the email address you used when purchasing your Masteriyo subscription.  
2. Navigate to the [License Management](https://docs.masteriyo.com/customer-account-management/customer-dashboard-manage-site-license#manage-site-license) section and deactivate the license associated with your site.  
3. Once the license is deactivated in the Customer Dashboard, go to your website's dashboard.  
4. Reactivate the license directly from your website's dashboard.  

This process will help sync your license correctly and ensure your site functions as expected. For detailed guidance on how to log into your customer dashboard and deactivate the license, go through this link: [Customer Dashboard - Manage Site License](https://docs.masteriyo.com/customer-account-management/customer-dashboard-manage-site-license)
