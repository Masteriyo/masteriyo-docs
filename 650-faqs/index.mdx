Start from the symptom. If something is broken, the fastest route is usually
[How to debug errors in Masteriyo](/faqs/debugging-error) — it tells you whether you are looking at a PHP error, a
permissions problem or a caching problem, which decides everything else. If a student paid and cannot get in, go
straight to [Student Can't Access a Course After Paying](/faqs/how-to-fix-course-access-issue).
