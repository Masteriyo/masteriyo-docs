## Overview

Recurring payments allow you to monetize your expertise through a subscription model, ensuring consistent revenue while giving learners ongoing access to your materials. The Recurring Payment feature allows you to sell your courses on a subscription basis (e.g., weekly, monthly, or yearly).

## Requirements

- **Masteriyo Pro Plan:** The recurring pricing feature is available in the Pro version and above.
- **Stripe Payment Gateway:** You must have Stripe configured to handle the automated billing cycles.
- **SSL Certificate:** Your site must be secured with HTTPS to process recurring transactions safely.

## Setup and Configuration

Once you have configured the Stripe gateway, you can create the course with subscription by following the steps below:

### Step 1: Access the Course Pricing

- Go to **Masteriyo > Courses** and select the course you wish to set the recurring payment.
- Click on the **Settings** tab.

  ![Course settings](https://mlcucdwjz8hi.i.optimole.com/w:auto/h:auto/q:auto/id:7a2ff2efdd23a39600c07c6a47154953/directUpload/Course-settings.png)

- Click on the **Pricing** tab on the left sidebar.

  ![Pricing tab](https://mlcucdwjz8hi.i.optimole.com/w:auto/h:auto/q:auto/id:e6e8ad9477bad256a3774eece25c8659/directUpload/Pricing-tab.png)

### Step 2: Enable Recurring Payments

- Set the pricing option to **Paid**.
- Then, click on the **Recurring** option.
- Configure Billing Details:
  - **Subscription Price:** Enter the amount charged per cycle.
  - **Billing Frequency:** Choose Per Day, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly.
  - **Subscription Length:** Define if the plan should Never Expire or end after a Fixed Amount of cycles.

![Recurring price options](https://mlcucdwjz8hi.i.optimole.com/w:auto/h:auto/q:auto/id:488422184fec5aeecc10283f6b164df6/directUpload/Recurring-price-options.png)

### Step 3: Save and Publish

- Click **Update** or **Publish** to save your pricing settings and activate the subscription on your course.

![Update option](https://mlcucdwjz8hi.i.optimole.com/w:auto/h:auto/q:auto/id:4b18b3c46c00b3459b01c8ae7d0ca3f5/directUpload/Update-option.png)
