---
title: How to create Facebook App ID and secret?
excerpt: Learn how to generate Facebook app ID and secret for the Social Login add-on.
---

This documentation will help you generate the Facebook App ID and secret required for integrating the Masteriyo Social Login add-on.

- Go to [Facebook Developer Console](https://developers.facebook.com/).
- Log in to your Facebook account and click on the **Create App** button.
- Select the app type/use case that matches your website and click **Next**.
- Give your app a name and complete the app creation steps.

Once your app is created, you'll be redirected to the app dashboard.

- From the **Add products to your app** section, find **Facebook Login** and click **Set Up**.
- Go to **Facebook Login > Settings** on the left sidebar.
- Enter the redirect URL given in **Masteriyo > Settings > Advanced > Social Login** in the **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs** field, then click **Save Changes**.
- Go to **App Settings > Basic** on the left sidebar to find your **App ID** and **App Secret**. Click **Show** next to App Secret to reveal it.

You can now use these credentials to integrate Facebook Login with Masteriyo. Please note that the Social Login add-on must be activated.
