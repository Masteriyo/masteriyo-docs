Sometimes, when you are trying to troubleshoot some issues, the version of your site’s PHP plays a significant role. We suspect that many of the users are unaware of the PHP version that they are using. This documentation is for assistance to those users. You can find steps to find out the PHP and WordPress versions of your site over here.

## PHP Version

Login to the site’s admin dashboard, and go to the following location:

        Dashboard > Tools > Site Health

![php-version](https://github.com/Masteriyo/masteriyo-docs/assets/75772539/67296a31-1500-41a7-8213-c0c94867ea28)

Now you’re on the Site Health page. There are two options **Status and Info**. Just click on **‘Info’**.

![site-health-info](https://github.com/Masteriyo/masteriyo-docs/assets/75772539/21cef7ed-b790-47b8-b57b-bb2241a4fcba)

Click on **Server**, and there you will find the PHP version.

![server-php-version](https://github.com/Masteriyo/masteriyo-docs/assets/75772539/41899d6d-496a-4c13-bd47-25ad731cb57c)

## WordPress Version

Similar to the PHP version, if your site is on WordPress 5.0 or higher, the easiest way to check the WordPress version is from the website's admin dashboard itself.

- Login to the site’s admin dashboard.
- Go to **Tools > Site Health**.
- Now you’re on the Site Health page. There are two options **Status and Info**. Just click on ‘Info’.
- Click on **WordPress**, and there you will find the WordPress version.

![wordpress-version](https://github.com/Masteriyo/masteriyo-docs/assets/75772539/f1a787d2-14a2-4f0b-a97c-2c3e64ddae7c)

        For the WordPress version, you can simply look at the bottom-right of the admin dashboard window.
