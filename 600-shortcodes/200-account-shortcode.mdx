title: Account Shortcode
excerpt: Create a login and account page with the [masteriyo_account] shortcode — shows the login form and the student account dashboard after sign-in.
---

The account shortcode is:  `[masteriyo_account]` \
This shortcode displays the login form when you are not logged in and shows the account page content after you are logged in.
