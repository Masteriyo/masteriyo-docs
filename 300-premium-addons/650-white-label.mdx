This feature lets you use your brand instead of the Masteriyo plugin. With this feature, you can hide the Masteriyo plugin's images and name and replace them with your own.

## Prerequisites
1. Install [Masteriyo LMS Elite plan](https://masteriyo.com/pricing/).
2. Activate the [White Label Add-on](/getting-started/activate-masteriyo-addons).

## Configuration
1. Go to your **Dashboard**, navigate to **Masteriyo** -> **Settings** -> **Advanced**.
2. Click on the **White Label**.
3. Add your Brand name in the **Title** section.
4. Upload your brand **Logo** and the **Active/Inactive Menu Icons**.
5. Click on **Update**.
