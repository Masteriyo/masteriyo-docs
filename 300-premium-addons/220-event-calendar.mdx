The Event Calendar feature in Masteriyo Pro shows the monthly schedule of assignments, quizzes, lessons, and Zoom meetings.

## Prerequisites

1. [Masteriyo LMS Pro plan or higher](https://masteriyo.com/pricing/).
2. [Active installation of Masteriyo LMS](/getting-started/installation#installing-masteriyo-lms-pro-version).

## Configuration

The Event Calendar is a built-in feature of Masteriyo Pro (it was migrated from an add-on to core in v3.1.0). Once Masteriyo Pro is active, a new **Calendar** tab is added to the students' account page. Go to the **Calendar** tab on the Account page to view all the upcoming events.

![calendar-tab](https://github.com/Masteriyo/masteriyo-docs/assets/75772539/587f4911-0817-44dd-a290-adf1efddb56b)

Click on each date to see the events for that day. You can also switch to any month and year as needed. Events include assignment dates, Zoom meetings (if integrated), etc.
