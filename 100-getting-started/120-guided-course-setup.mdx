## What is Guided Course Setup?

Right after you activate Masteriyo LMS for the first time, you're automatically taken through a short setup wizard that configures only what your site actually needs — whether you're selling courses, taking payments, or just exploring.

<Callout>
  Location: automatically opens after activation, at **Masteriyo → Onboard**
  (`wp-admin/admin.php?page=masteriyo-onboard`). The redirect only fires once,
  right after activation — if you close it or navigate away, you can return to
  it any time at that URL, and it picks up right where you left off instead of
  restarting.
</Callout>

The wizard has four steps: **Welcome**, **Payments**, **Starter Templates**, and **Finish**.

## Step 1: Welcome

The wizard opens with a single question:

> **How will learners get access to your courses?**

- They will buy courses online
- We will enroll them ourselves
- Both
- I am just exploring

Your answer decides whether the **Payments** step appears next — pick "I am just exploring" or "We will enroll them ourselves" and the wizard skips straight to Starter Templates.

This step also includes a usage-tracking consent checkbox: *"Help us improve Masteriyo by sharing non-sensitive diagnostic and usage data."* — see [Allow Usage Tracking](/getting-started/allow-usage-tracking) for what's included.

![Guided setup Welcome step](https://img.masteriyo.com/w:auto/h:auto/q:auto/id:7c30d54a3b6b06165838a13f74e24626/directUpload/welcome.png)

## Step 2: Payments

Shown only if you indicated you'll be selling courses. Fields:

- **Currency** — a dropdown pre-selected with a best guess based on your site's domain
- **Offline Payment** — a simple on/off toggle, no extra fields
- **PayPal** — toggle on to reveal a **PayPal Email** field for your PayPal business account
- **Stripe** — toggle on to activate the Stripe add-on (if not already active) and connect your account via the **Connect with Stripe** button

<Callout>
  Additional payment gateways can always be set up later from **Masteriyo →
  Settings → Payments**.
</Callout>

![Guided setup Payments step](https://img.masteriyo.com/w:auto/h:auto/q:auto/id:8750a0c89279d7c78e2ee5e85aa93700/directUpload/payments.png)

## Step 3: Starter Templates

This step reuses the same [Starter Templates](/settings/starter-templates) picker available elsewhere in the plugin. You can:

- **Skip** — move straight to Finish without importing anything
- **Continue** — pick a builder (Elementor or Gutenberg) and a demo template, then configure typography/colors and course display settings for the imported demo

A **Courses Page** preview card is shown as part of this configuration step.

## Step 4: Finish

A summary of what's ready, which varies slightly based on your earlier answers — for example, whether your Courses, Learn, Account, and Checkout pages are all set up (or just Courses, Learn, and Account, if you're not taking payments).

### Sample Course

A **sample course** toggle is on by default:

- **On, fresh site** — three example courses are added to your library as drafts
- **On, existing courses already present** — two example courses are added instead
- **Off, fresh site** — a single "Getting Started with Masteriyo" course is still added

![Guided setup Finish step](https://img.masteriyo.com/w:auto/h:auto/q:auto/id:d9771e6c3cc71c036b06093fff8ffc22/directUpload/finish.png)

Two buttons finish the wizard:

- **Go to your courses** — takes you to **Masteriyo → Courses**
- **Visit your site** — takes you to your site's homepage

## Skipping Steps

- Every step has a **Skip this step** link, letting you move on without filling anything in (except Welcome, where you must pick an access answer first — the answer is kept even if you skip the rest of that step's fields)
- A **×** close icon in the header (on every step except Finish) exits the wizard entirely and sends you to your WordPress homepage, **without saving** whatever you were mid-way through configuring

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if I close the wizard partway through?

Just revisit `wp-admin/admin.php?page=masteriyo-onboard` — it resumes at the step you left off on, using what you already answered.

### Does skipping payments mean I can't sell courses later?

No. You can configure any payment gateway at any time from **Masteriyo → Settings → Payments**, regardless of what you chose during setup.

### Can I turn off the sample courses after they're created?

Yes, they're just regular draft courses — delete or edit them like any other course from **Masteriyo → Courses**.

### Does the wizard run again after a plugin update?

No, only once, right after the very first activation.
