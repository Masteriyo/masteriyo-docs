## Installing Masteriyo LMS (Learning Management System) - Free version

<Embed>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-yx_4Eunig0</Embed>

- Login to your WordPress website.
- In your site's dashboard, go to **Plugins** -> **Add New**.
- In the search area, type **Masteriyo** and hit Enter.
- Once you find the Masteriyo LMS plugin, click on the **Install Now** button.
- Click on the **Activate** button.

## Installing Masteriyo LMS - Pro version

<Embed>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SP5ooVwO0W8</Embed>

Once you purchase Masteriyo Pro, you’ll receive an email that includes the downloadable link and license key. Follow the below steps to download and activate it:

### Downloading Masteriyo Pro

- Click the **Download Masteriyo Pro** button, which redirects you to the order page.
- On the order page, the older version plugin is listed at the top and the newer version at the bottom.
- Now **download** the latest version of the plugin.

<Callout>
  {" "} 
  **Note:** Do not forget to copy the License Key from your email, which you need
  for activating the Masteriyo Pro features.
</Callout>

### Installing Masteriyo Pro

- Go to your WordPress dashboard. Then, navigate to **Plugins** -> **Add New**
- Click on **Upload Plugin** and press the **Choose File** button.
- Upload the zip file of Masteriyo Pro that you downloaded.
- Click on **Install Now** and press the **Activate Plugin** button.
- Go back to your dashboard, and navigate to **Masteriyo** -> **License**.
- Paste the license key that you copied earlier.
- Click on the **Activate** button.

<Callout>
  {" "} 
  **Note:** If you’ve already used the free version of Masteriyo, activating Masteriyo
  Pro deactivates its free version automatically.{" "}
</Callout>
